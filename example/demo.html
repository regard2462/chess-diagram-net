Chess Diagram Net

Reads the position from a picture of a printed chess diagram – a scan, a photo crop, a screenshot or a whole book page. Everything runs on this page; no picture is uploaded anywhere.

The squares with an orange frame are the ones the network is least sure about. The position is given from White's side; pick who is to move before you open it in an analysis board. Source code and training (AGPL-3.0)
